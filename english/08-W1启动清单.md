## 二、基线自测：10 句（9/14 做）

**做法**：每句只写两个数字 —— **谓语个数** 和 **连接手段个数**，然后算 `差值`。
**不要翻译，不要分层，不要查词。** 限时 15 分钟做完 10 句。

```
1. Researchers who study sleep argue that the habit of checking a phone
   before bed disrupts the body's natural rhythm.

2. The report released last month suggests that policies designed to
   reduce emissions have had little effect on the industries that
   produce them.

3. What many parents fail to realise is that the pressure they place on
   their children often produces the opposite of what they intend.

4. Although the technology has existed for decades, it was not until
   costs fell sharply that manufacturers began to adopt it on a large
   scale.

5. The assumption that economic growth will automatically lift people
   out of poverty ignores the fact that the benefits are rarely
   distributed evenly.

6. Scientists have long suspected that the bacteria found in soil
   samples collected from deep caves may hold the key to antibiotics
   that current drugs cannot replace.

7. Not only did the reform fail to reduce inequality, but it also
   widened the gap between the regions that had already prospered and
   those that had not.

8. The extent to which a language shapes the way its speakers perceive
   the world remains a subject of debate among linguists who otherwise
   agree on very little.

9. Had the committee anticipated how strongly the proposal would be
   opposed, it would almost certainly have postponed the vote until the
   objections that surfaced later could be addressed.

10. That so few of the students whom the programme was designed to help
    actually completed it suggests either that the criteria were poorly
    chosen or that the support promised was never delivered.
```

## 三、答案与陷阱（做完再看）

| # | 谓语 | 谓语列表 | 连接 | 连接列表 | 差 | 陷阱 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | study / argue / disrupts | 2 | who, that | 1 | `checking` 是动名词，**不是**谓语 |
| 2 | 3 | suggests / have had / produce | 2 | that, that | 1 | `released`、`designed` 都是分词定语 |
| 3 | 5 | fail / is / place / produces / intend | 4 | What, that, **∅**, what | 1 | `the pressure ∅ they place` **省略关系代词** |
| 4 | 4 | has existed / was / fell / began | 3 | Although, until, that | 1 | `it was not until...that` 是**强调结构**，that 算连接 |
| 5 | 3 | will lift / ignores / are distributed | 2 | that, that | 1 | 两个 that 一个是同位语从句、一个也是同位语从句 |
| 6 | 3 | have suspected / may hold / cannot replace | 2 | that, that | 1 | `found`、`collected` **两个分词连排**，都不是谓语 |
| 7 | 4 | did fail / widened / had prospered / had | 3 | but, that, that | 1 | `Not only` 引起**倒装**；`those that had not` 后面省略了 prospered |
| 8 | 4 | shapes / perceive / remains / agree | 3 | to which, **∅**, who | 1 | 主语 `The extent...world` 和谓语 `remains` 隔了 **14 个词**；`the way ∅ its speakers perceive` 省略了 that |
| 9 | 5 | Had anticipated / would be opposed / would have postponed / surfaced / could be addressed | 4 | **Had**(=if), how, until, that | 1 | **虚拟语气倒装省略 if**，`Had` 就是那个连接手段 |
| 10 | 5 | was designed / completed / suggests / were chosen / was delivered | 4 | That, whom, that, **or that**(算1个) | 1 | `or that` **合计算 1 个**（见 `01` 计数细则）；`promised` 是分词；主语从句 `That...it` 长达 13 词 |

### 怎么读你的分数

| 10 句里算对 | 说明 | W1 怎么调 |
|---|---|---|
| **9-10 句** | 谓语识别已经过关 | 直接进 W2，加分层练习 |
| **6-8 句** | 典型水平，符合预期 | 按原计划，每天 10 句 |
| **3-5 句** | 非谓语识别是主要问题 | 每天加 10 分钟**专项**：只做"这个 -ing/-ed 是不是谓语"的判断，不看句子 |
| **0-2 句** | 先补基础，别硬推 | 告诉我，我出一份非谓语专项（3 天） |

📌 **看错在哪一类比看对几个重要**。把错的题号记进错误日志，标错因编码（1-5）。
第 3、8、9 题错 → 省略问题（编码 2）；第 1、2、6 题错 → 非谓语问题（编码 1）；
第 8 题错 → 主谓隔开（编码 3）。**这三类就是你 W2-4 的主攻方向。**
